# Google Search Console Indexing & Dynamic Sitemap Guide

This guide walks you through the exact steps to submit your Next.js + Headless WordPress dynamic sitemap to Google Search Console. By doing this, you will ensure Google crawls all your static landing pages, image portfolios, and newly published blog posts in real-time.

---

## 🚀 The Dynamic URL Structure

Because we built a server-side dynamic sitemap in Next.js, your sitemap is automatically generated on the fly.

* **Sitemap Location:** `https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml`
* **Real-time Crawling:** Googlebot fetches the sitemap directly. Every time you publish or update a blog post in your WordPress dashboard, this XML feed automatically updates without you needing to re-build or re-deploy the site!

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Add Your Property in Google Search Console

1. Navigate to the **[Google Search Console Dashboard](https://search.google.com/search-console)**.
2. Sign in with your primary Google account.
3. In the top-left property selector, click **Add Property**.
4. You will be prompted to choose a Property Type:
   * **Option A: Domain (Recommended)**
     * Enter your domain (e.g., `ishasigns.com`).
     * *Note:* This covers both `http`, `https`, `www`, and all subdomains.
   * **Option B: URL Prefix**
     * Enter the exact URL of your production site (e.g., `https://ishasigns.com`).

---

## 🔑 Step 2: Verify Domain Ownership

Google must verify that you own the site. Choose one of the standard methods:

### Method A: DNS TXT Record (Highly Recommended)
1. Copy the **TXT record** generated by Google Search Console.
2. Log into your domain registrar (e.g., GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare).
3. Open your Domain’s **DNS Management** settings.
4. Add a new record:
   * **Type:** `TXT`
   * **Host/Name:** `@` (or leave blank depending on your registrar)
   * **Value:** *[Paste the Google verification code here]*
   * **TTL:** `3600` or `Automatic`
5. Save the DNS record, return to Google Search Console, and click **Verify**. *(Note: DNS changes can take a few minutes to propagate).*

### Method B: HTML Tag (Next.js Setup)
If you choose the URL Prefix method, you can verify via an HTML meta tag:
1. Copy the meta tag (e.g., `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="XYZ..." />`).
2. Add this metadata key directly into your main Next.js App Router root layout `src/app/layout.tsx`:
   ```typescript
   export const metadata: Metadata = {
     title: 'ISHA Signage',
     // ...
     verification: {
       google: 'XYZ...', // Paste only the verification code here
     }
   };
   ```
3. Deploy the updated Next.js app and click **Verify**.

---

## 🗺️ Step 3: Submit Your Dynamic Sitemap

Once your domain is verified, you are ready to let Google index the site!

1. In the left sidebar of Google Search Console, under the **Indexing** tab, click on **Sitemaps**.
2. Under the **Add a new sitemap** input field, you will see your domain already pre-filled.
3. Type in the exact name of your sitemap:
   ```text
   sitemap.xml
   ```
4. Click **Submit**.

---

## 📈 Step 4: Validate crawls & Monitor Status

* **Instant Confirmation:** Google will show a notice saying *"Sitemap submitted successfully"*.
* **Status Checks:** In the sitemap table, check the **Status** column:
  * **Success (Green):** Google has read the sitemap successfully and detected all URLs (e.g., `sitemap.xml` will show your Static pages + WordPress dynamic posts counts).
  * **Fetched successfully:** Shows that the sitemap is active.
* **Regular Recrawling:** You do not need to resubmit the sitemap again! Googlebot will periodically request `https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml` (usually daily or weekly) to find new URLs.

---

## 💡 Pro SEO Tips for Headless Setup

1. **Verify your NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL:** Ensure that your environment variables set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to your live domain (e.g., `https://ishasigns.com`). This ensures the sitemap URLs match your public domain exactly.
2. **Inspect Specific URLs:** Use the **URL Inspection Tool** (search bar at the top of Search Console) to inspect `/images` or any dynamic `/blog/slug` page to test how Google renders it.
3. **Internal Linking:** Ensure your blog posts contain high-quality internal links back to your homepage (`/`) or inquiry form to boost spatial design authority!
